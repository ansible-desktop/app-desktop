0.3.0 (20.09.26)

- Rebase onto upstream Telegram Desktop 7.2.8, API layer 229.
- Russian interface, served by the Ansible backend: 7683 of 7683 strings.
- Rebrand user-visible English strings to Ansible.
- Rebrand translation key names: telegram to ansible, stars to diamonds,
  star to diamond, credits to diamonds. 368 keys, applied to the client
  and to the language packs in one wave.
- Crash reporting is enabled again and now reports to our own receiver at
  api.ansible.su/crash instead of upstream.
- Keep the star shape in the photo editor and the Gram balance strings
  under their own names: those are not the diamond currency.
- Single data centre and our own RSA key for the handshake; own api_id.
- Links are asme.su, deep links are as://, animated stickers are .ass.
  Telegram's t.me, tg:// and .tgs are no longer recognised.
- Forums are available in any group, without the member threshold.
- Accept a phone number typed with spaces, brackets or dashes.
- Drop the legacy ~/.TelegramDesktop lookup on Linux: a leftover folder
  from Telegram Desktop is no longer read.
- Own installer identifier, so Ansible Desktop and Telegram Desktop no
  longer overwrite each other's install entry on Windows.
- Version is now set from one place and cannot drift between the
  executable, the installer and the running application.

Earlier releases are listed at
https://github.com/ansible-desktop/app-desktop/releases
